# Evaluation Rubric: C-Suite Strategic Memo & Proposal Writing (Word)

## Overview
Evaluates the generation of executive briefing memos, investment committee papers, and business cases for Fortune 500 leadership.

---

## 1. Scoring Dimensions

| Dimension | Weight | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **BLUF & Executive Summary** | 30% | Immediate declaration of the core decision, capital requirement, ROI, and timeline within the first 150 words. |
| **Strategic Logic & Depth** | 25% | Rigorous exploration of risks, mitigations, alternatives considered, and downside sensitivity. |
| **Tone & Executive Register** | 20% | Objective, authoritative, precise, and devoid of filler/marketing hype. |
| **Document Architecture** | 15% | Clean header hierarchy (H1 -> H2 -> H3), callout call boxes for key metrics, structured comparison tables. |
| **Constraint Adherence** | 10% | Strict adherence to word counts, required sections, and explicit formatting constraints. |

---

## 2. Rubric Grading Grid
* **Grade A (4.5 - 5.0)**: Ready for CEO review with zero structural edits. Quantified metrics, clear trade-offs, balanced assessment.
* **Grade B (3.0 - 4.4)**: Strong content, but lacks punchy executive framing or uses overly verbose narrative paragraphs.
* **Grade C (1.0 - 2.9)**: Generic AI output with boilerplate conclusions, missing risk quantification, or incomplete sections.
